Notes from the framework

How a Pune developer turned bin/rails notes into a page every Rails 8.0 app ships with, and why he keeps fixing other people's code.

Open /rails/info/notes in any Rails 8.0 app and you get a table of every TODO, FIXME and OPTIMIZE in the codebase. It sits next to /rails/info/routes, and it's there because of rails/rails#49240.

localhost:3000/rails/info/notes
FileLineTagDescription
app/models/user.rb12TODOMove to a concern
app/jobs/sync_job.rb4FIXMERetry on timeout
app/queries/report.rb27OPTIMIZEN+1 on line items
